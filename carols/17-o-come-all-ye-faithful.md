## Carol: O Come, All Ye Faithful

*Please stand, if you are able, to sing*

<div style="text-align: center;">
<strong>
O come, all ye faithful, Joyful and triumphant,<br/>
O come ye, O come ye to Bethlehem;<br/>
Come and behold him, Born the king of Angels:<br/>
<em>O come, let us adore Him,<br/>
O come, let us adore Him,<br/>
O come, let us adore Him,<br/>
Christ the lord!</em><br/>
<br/>
God of God, Light of Light,<br/>
Lo! He abhors not the Virgin's womb;<br/>
Very god, Begotten not created:<br/>
<em>O come, let us adore Him …</em><br/>
<br/>
See how the shepherds, Summoned to his Cradle,<br/>
Leaving their flocks, draw nigh with lowly fear;<br/>
We too will thither, Bend our joyful footsteps<br/>
<em>O come, let us adore Him …</em><br/>
<br/>
Sing, choirs of angels, Sing in exultation,<br/>
Sing, all ye citizens of heav’n above;<br/>
Glory to God, In the highest:<br/>
<em>O come, let us adore Him …</em><br/>
</strong>
</div>

*Please remain standing for the final blessing*
