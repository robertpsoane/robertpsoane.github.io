## Carol: Creator of the Stars of Night

*Please remain seated and listen as the choir sing*

<div style="text-align: center;">
Creator of the stars of night,<br/>
Your people's everlasting light,<br/>
Jesu, Redeemer, save us all,<br/>
And hear your servants when they call.<br/>
<br/>
When Earth was near its evening hour,<br/>
You did, in love’s redeeming pow’r,<br/>
Like bridegroom from his chamber, come<br/>
Forth from a virgin mother’s womb.<br/>
<br/>
At your great name, exalted now,<br/>
All knees in lowly homage bow;<br/>
All things in Heav’n and Earth adore<br/>
And own you King forevermore.<br/>
<br/>
To you, O Holy One, we pray,<br/>
Our Judge in that tremendous day,<br/>
Ward off, while yet we dwell below,<br/>
The weapons of our crafty foe.<br/>
<br/>
To God the Father, God the Son,<br/>
And God the Spirit, Three in One,<br/>
Praise, honor, might, and glory be,<br/>
From age to age eternally.<br/>
</div>
