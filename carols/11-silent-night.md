## Carol: Silent Night

*Please stand, if you are able, to sing*

<div style="text-align: center;">
<strong>
Silent night, holy night,<br/>
All is calm, all is bright;<br/>
Round yon virgin mother and child.<br/>
Holy infant so tender and mild,<br/>
Sleep in heavenly peace (x2)<br/>
<br/>
Silent night, holy night,<br/>
Shepherds first saw the sight:<br/>
Glories stream from heaven afar,<br/>
Heav’nly hosts sing alleluia:<br/>
Christ the saviour is born (x2)<br/>
<br/>
Silent night, holy night,<br/>
Son of god, loves pure light<br/>
Radians beams from thy holy face,<br/>
WIth the dawn of redeeming grade,<br/>
Jesus, Lord, at thy birth, (x2)<br/>
</strong>
</div>

*Please sit*
