## Carol: Lo! He Comes with Coulds Descending

*Please stand, if you are able, to sing*

<div style="text-align: center;">
<strong>
Lo he comes with clouds descending<br/>
Once for all sinners slain<br/>
Thousand thousand saints descending<br/>
Swell the triumph of his train<br/>
Alleluia!<br/>
Alleluia!<br/>
Alleluia!<br/>
God appears on earth to reign!<br/>
<br/>
Every eye shall now behold him<br/>
Robed in dreadful majesty<br/>
Those who set at nought and sold him<br/>
Pierced and nailed him to a tree<br/>
Deeply wailing,<br/>
Deeply wailing,<br/>
Deeply wailing,<br/>
Shall the true Messiah see.<br/>
<br/>
Those dear tokens of his passion<br/>
Still his dazzling body bears<br/>
Cause of endless Exultation<br/>
To his ransomed worshippers<br/>
With what rapture,<br/>
With what rapture,<br/>
With what rapture,<br/>
Gaze we on those glorious scars!<br/>
<br/>
Yes, amen! Let all adore thee,<br/>
High on thine eternal throne<br/>
Saviour, take the power and glory;<br/>
Claim the kingdom for thine own<br/>
O come quickly!<br/>
O come quickly!<br/>
O come quickly!<br/>
Alleluia! Come, Lord, Come!<br/>
</strong>
</div>

*Please sit*
